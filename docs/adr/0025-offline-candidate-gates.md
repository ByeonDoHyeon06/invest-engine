# ADR-0025 — Research registration and unknown-gap boundary

Status: Accepted offline foundation (#44 verified); E6 release not accepted.

Preserve strict minute evaluation; expose actual absent-time intervals and aggregate coverage, do not infer zero trade bars or execute across gaps. Add immutable research plans and evidence snapshots separate from TradingCore. Bind input identities and temporal split, compare matched cash and executable hold controls under multiple cost assumptions. Separate validation selection and one selected holdout evaluation; unverified inputs are inconclusive. Caller-side durable one-use registry, evidence attestation, true holdout isolation, feature-level purge, and independent E5 acceptance are outstanding. Research pass grants no execution rights.

See [E5.15c / E6 foundation](../E5_15C_E6_FOUNDATION.md).
